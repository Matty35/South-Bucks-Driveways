Driveway repairs & restoration

Not every driveway needs replacing. We repair, re-level and restore existing block paving, gravel and resin surfaces — and fix the drainage problems that caused the damage in the first place.

Describe your repair

What we repair

  • Sunken or rutted areas lifted and re-laid on a rebuilt sub-base
  • Block paving re-sanding, re-jointing and sealing
  • Loose or spreading edges re-set on proper haunching
  • Ponding and run-off problems — falls, channels and soakaway remedials
  • Worn gravel topped up, contained and re-edged

Repairs and the planning rules

Repairing or replacing up to 5 m² of existing hard surfacing doesn't require permeable materials or permission. Beyond that, like-for-like repair is usually fine — but if you're enlarging or resurfacing a front garden, the permeability rules apply. We'll flag it honestly either way.

Fix the cause, not just the surface

Most driveway failure in this area is a drainage or sub-base problem wearing a surface-problem disguise. Our repair visits start below the blocks: what the base is, where the water goes, and why it moved. Then we quote for a repair that lasts.

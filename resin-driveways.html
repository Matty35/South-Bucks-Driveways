Resin bound driveways

A permeable, UV-stable surface that suits both period frontages and modern homes — and, installed correctly, stays within permitted development on most South Buckinghamshire properties.

Resin bound, not resin bonded

The distinction matters legally as well as practically. Resin bound surfacing mixes aggregate through the resin and is porous, so rain drains through it — which is why it normally needs no planning permission, whatever the size. Resin bonded scatter coats are impermeable: on a front garden over 5 m² they trigger the planning rules unless drainage to a permeable area is engineered in.

We install fully permeable resin bound systems with UV-stable resin, over a free-draining sub-base designed for your ground.

Built from the ground up

  • Full dig-out to a specified depth, not an overlay by default
  • Free-draining sub-base engineered for SUDS compliance
  • Soakaways and threshold channels where the frontage needs them
  • UV-stable resin and locally sympathetic aggregate blends

Suited to the Chilterns

Bound aggregate finishes are one of the surfacing approaches favoured by Chilterns design guidance — quieter and more natural than patterned concrete or bright paviours, and a good match for conservation-area streets in Beaconsfield, the Chalfonts and Amersham. We help you choose blends that sit well against brick, flint and render.

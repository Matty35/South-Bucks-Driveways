# Photo drop-off folder

Drop your project photos in here (straight from your phone is fine — JPG, PNG or HEIC).
Then ask Claude to "process the photos in photos-inbox" and they'll be:

1. Converted to WebP and compressed for the web,
2. Given proper filenames, alt text, and width/height attributes,
3. Moved into /images/ and placed on the site (galleries, case studies, og:image),
4. Cleared out of this folder once live.

## What helps most (from the competitor research)

The strongest local ranking pattern is a photo tied to a real place and job, so if you can:

- **Name the town in the filename** if it's quick, e.g. `resin-beaconsfield-1.jpg`,
  `block-paving-chalfont-st-peter-before.jpg` — or just tell Claude which job is which.
- **Before AND after shots** of the same driveway are gold — competitors barely have any.
- **Mid-job photos** (dig-out, sub-base, grids, drainage) prove the build-up we talk about
  on every page — nobody else shows this.
- A straight-on shot of the finished drive **with the house frontage in view** works best
  for galleries and social sharing.

## Practicalities

- Keep individual files under ~20MB (GitHub blocks files over 100MB; originals from a
  phone are fine).
- Don't worry about editing, cropping or compressing — that happens in processing.
- Anything in this folder is temporary staging: it will deploy with the site until
  processed, so avoid photos you don't want public.

A local firm, built on local ground

South Bucks Resin & Driveways installs and restores driveways for period and modern homes across South Buckinghamshire — the towns and villages of the Chilterns we know street by street.

How we work

Every job starts with a proper site visit: the ground, the drainage, the frontage's setting, and any conservation-area or Article 4 constraints that apply to your street. You get an itemised written quotation — dig-out depth, sub-base specification, drainage provision, edging and surface — so you can compare like with like.

We do the groundwork ourselves and we don't hide it in the price, because the groundwork is the driveway.

What stands behind the work

  • Written, insurance-backed workmanship guarantee on every installation
  • Full public liability insurance
  • Permeable, SUDS-compliant construction as standard
  • Local references available on request

Gravel driveways

The traditional surface of the Chilterns' larger frontages — naturally permeable, quietly elegant, and engineered properly so it doesn't migrate, rut or sink.

Request a quotation

Right for large and carriage frontages

Gerrards Cross and Beaconsfield are full of wide, sweeping frontages where gravel is the natural choice — it's fully permeable (so no planning permission at any size), it suits period settings, and bound-gravel finishes are explicitly encouraged by Chilterns design guidance in place of large areas of tarmac.

Engineered, not just tipped

  • Compacted sub-base with geotextile separation — no mud pumping through
  • Gravel-retention grids on drives and slopes that need them
  • Angular, self-binding or resin-bound gravel options by use
  • Defined edges in brick, setts or steel to keep stone off the road

Conservation-area friendly

In conservation areas and Article 4 streets, gravel is often the lowest-friction choice: permeable, traditional in appearance, and sympathetic to period frontages. We advise on stone colour and size so the drive sits naturally against flint, brick and mature planting.

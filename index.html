Planning-aware from the first visit

Much of South Buckinghamshire sits inside the Chilterns National Landscape, and towns like Beaconsfield Old Town and the Chalfonts include conservation areas and Article 4 directions that change what you can build without permission. Front-garden surfacing over 5 m² must be permeable or drain to a permeable area to stay within permitted development.

We design every driveway around those rules — materials, appearance and drainage — and tell you plainly when a planning application is needed.

Drainage designed, not assumed

Large frontages need real drainage design: correctly specified permeable sub-base build-ups, soakaways sized to the ground conditions, and channel drainage at thresholds. Chalk and clay behave differently — we build for the ground you have.

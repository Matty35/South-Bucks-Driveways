Standard or permeable — know the difference

Conventional block paving is an impermeable surface. On a front garden larger than 5 m² that means planning permission, unless rainwater is directed to a permeable area or you choose permeable block paving — a distinct product laid on an open-graded sub-base that lets water drain through the joints. We'll tell you which route fits your frontage before you commit.

Specified for period streets

Chilterns design guidance steers away from highly coloured paviours, fake cobbles and patterned concrete. We work with muted, natural block ranges and traditional laying patterns that complement brick-and-flint frontages in Beaconsfield Old Town, the Chalfonts and Amersham.

Built to stay flat

  • Dig-out and sub-base compacted to depth, matched to vehicle loads
  • Edge restraints on concrete haunching — no creeping borders
  • Drainage falls, channels and soakaways designed in from the start
  • Jointing sand and sealing options explained, with aftercare guidance
